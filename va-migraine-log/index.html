What the rating schedule says

The VA rates migraine under 38 CFR § 4.124a, Diagnostic Code 8100. This is the published wording:

50%: With very frequent completely prostrating and prolonged attacks productive of severe economic inadaptability
30%: With characteristic prostrating attacks occurring on an average once a month over last several months
10%: With characteristic prostrating attacks averaging one in 2 months over last several months
0%: With less frequent attacks

The wording turns on how often attacks happen, whether they are prostrating, how long they last, and at 50% their economic impact. How VA applies those words is for VA and your representative.

The regulation itself doesn't define "prostrating". So this log doesn't use the word on your entries. It asks a plain question you can answer honestly on the day: did you have to stop what you were doing and lie down?

What you log

  • the date, and whether it was a migraine or a headache
  • worst pain, 0 to 10
  • whether you had to stop and lie down
  • about how many hours it lasted
  • whether you missed work or school
  • what you took, and a short note in your own words

Log the clear days as well, with one tap. A log that shows the good days and the bad days reads as a real record. Skipped days are marked "not logged", never counted as migraine-free.

Log what happened, on the day it happened. A record kept as you go is worth more than one rebuilt from memory, and an honest log is the only kind worth handing to an examiner.

Straight answers

Are you part of the VA?

No. This is an independent record-keeping tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

Do you help file claims?

No. We don't prepare, file, review or advise on claims, and this isn't legal advice. VA-recognized Veterans Service Organizations such as DAV, VFW and the American Legion have accredited representatives who help for free. Find one on VA.gov.

Do I need to pay for a log at all?

No. A paper log is fine, and ours is free to print. The paid version is for people who would rather tap a phone than keep a sheet of paper for six months, and want the totals added up.

How long should I keep it?

The schedule talks about averages "over last several months", so a few months of entries says more than a few weeks. Ask your VSO what they would like to see for your situation.

Where does my data go?

Your entries are saved only in your browser on your own phone. We never receive them. The web host that delivers the page sees your IP address, like any website, and anyone who can unlock your phone can open the log.

Your log can be erased if you clear browsing data, use private mode, switch browser or phone, or (on iPhone) don't open it for a while. The app reminds you to download a backup; do it weekly.
