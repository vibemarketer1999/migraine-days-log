Privacy policy

The short version: if you don't turn on sync, your log never leaves your phone. If you do, it's stored on Google Firebase so you can get it back, it is never sold or shared, and you can delete it in one tap.

Effective 19 November 2026. This policy also serves as our consumer health data privacy policy.

Who we are

Migraine Days Log is a small independent product made and run by one person, Fiona. "We" and "I" in this policy both mean her. It is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical service, not a covered entity under HIPAA, and not affiliated with the Department of Veterans Affairs.

If you don't turn on sync

Your entries are saved only in your browser, on your device. We never receive them. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. The only time the app contacts another company is if you choose to: for example, when you use "Ask your AI" and paste your entries into ChatGPT or Claude yourself, which sends them to that company under its own policy.

If you turn on sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on and tick the consent box. When it's on, we store:

  • your email address, so you can sign in with an emailed link instead of a password;
  • your log entries: which days were clear, headache or migraine days, pain scores, how long attacks lasted, whether you took medication and its name, whether you had to lie down or missed work or school, and any notes you wrote;
  • the preventive treatments you listed, your chosen starting period, and whether you have unlocked the full log;
  • the times your entries were last changed, so two devices don't overwrite each other.

This is health information, and we treat it that way. We use it for one purpose only: to sync your log between your devices and let you restore it. We do not sell it, share it, use it for advertising, or use it to train anything.

Where it's stored, and who can see it

Synced data is stored with Google Firebase (Google LLC) in data centres in the United States. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest and processes it on our behalf. Database rules allow your account to read and write only your own log.

I run the service, so I can technically open the database. I don't look at anyone's entries, unless you ask me to help with a problem and say I can, or the law requires it.

Firebase also handles the technical data needed to sign you in and keep the service secure, such as your IP address and device information. Google describes this in its own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • See your data: everything stored is what you see in the app. "Save a copy" and the exports give you the full file at any time.
  • Delete it: in the app, go to More and tap "Delete my synced data and account". That removes your synced log and your sign-in account. The log on your phone stays unless you delete it too.
  • Withdraw consent: deleting your synced data and account withdraws it. You can keep using the app without sync.
  • Questions or requests: reply to the email receipt from your purchase. We answer within 30 days. Seeing, saving and deleting your data you can do yourself in the app, any time, without asking.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. When you delete your synced data and account, it is removed from the live database straight away. If an account isn't used for three years, we may delete it after emailing you first.

If something goes wrong

If your synced data is ever exposed without your permission, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires, including under the US Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule.

Purchases

The $19 full log is sold through Payhip, and payments are handled by PayPal. They receive your name, email and payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive your email and order details from Payhip. We don't receive your card details.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records basic server logs such as IP addresses to keep the service running. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. The search engine Google may list our pages; that doesn't involve your data.

Children

Migraine Days Log is not meant for children under 13, and sync is not available to them. If you're a parent or guardian and believe a child has turned on sync, open the app on that device, go to More and tap "Delete my synced data and account".

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how synced data is used, we'll update the date above and ask for your consent again in the app before it applies to you.

Not medical advice

The log counts what you enter. It doesn't diagnose, interpret or suggest treatment. If a headache is sudden, severe or different from your usual, get medical help.
